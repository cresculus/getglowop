01 · OpEx vs CapEx

Repair hits the P&L

Battery and disk work is often deductible OpEx in the year you spend it. A truck of new laptops is usually capitalized and depreciated. The GLOW call shows which serials should stay on the OpEx path.

02 · Carbon cost

Pay less where carbon is priced

Where a carbon tax or emissions price applies, emitting less (or buying fewer manufactured devices) reduces what you owe per ton. Even in the U.S., documented avoided manufacturing CO₂e supports ESG packs and CPA conversations about credits and incentives that actually fit your facts.
