Calendar versus carbon — the screening math

Over 15 years, a 3-year refresh of 150 devices buys about 750 machines. Keeping the healthy 70% for 5 years buys about 540. The difference is manufacturing you skip: 210 devices · 46.0 t CO₂e · $231,000 at 219 kg and $1,100 per device. This is screening arithmetic with its assumptions printed on it — not a certified footprint. The report above does it per serial number.

How this report was made

1 · IngestIntune, RMM, or thin agent. SCCM-friendly when you need it. CSV for pilots.
2 · EnrichMatch to a manufacturer PCF or Boavizta’s open compilation. Fallbacks are labelled.
3 · DecideRules first. Security and a dead board still win over carbon.
4 · ReportOne report per client. Portfolio view for the MSP. PDF + CSV, white-label.

Public vs. proprietary, plainly: Manufacturer PCFs, Boavizta’s compilation, and the client’s own Intune / RMM / agent inventory are public / client data — cited, not claimed. The GLOW Score weights, the call, match confidence, persona windows, ghost rule, and calendar-vs-carbon comparison are the GLOW Op.

Scope — what this is not

Not a full GHG Protocol inventory. Not an assurance opinion. Not a reason to skip a security patch. Scope 3 language stays inside what the factors support. Phase 0: no production connector and no paying tenant yet — this is a sample report design, not a live tenant export.
