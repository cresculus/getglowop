GLOW Score™

GLOW Score is the Green Lifecycle score (0–100) inside GLOW Ops. It rates how long a device can stay in productive service before refresh — using age, check-in, health, and persona.

Day one is a screening call. Keep, ghost, and an obvious dead chassis can show up on the first upload. Which part to replace — battery, disk, or leave the chassis — is the detailed result. That needs about 30 days of agent check-ins so the report is a trend, not one snapshot. Tell the customer that clock starts when the agent is installed.

  • 60–100 Keep — extend use. The calendar is early.
  • 40–70 Repair — fix the battery or a part. Do not buy a new laptop yet.
  • Redeploy — storage is the failure and the chassis is still sound. No fixed band; the call comes from health, not the number alone.
  • 0–45 Refresh — plan a controlled replacement.
  • 0–30 Recycle — the chassis is done. Remarket or certified recycle.
  • 0–25 Ghost — it has not checked in. Find it before you buy its replacement.
  • Tax & spend — repair / keep is often near-term OpEx; calendar refresh is CapEx. Documented avoided manufacturing CO₂e supports CPA conversations where carbon is priced or incentives apply. Not tax advice.
