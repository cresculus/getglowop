07 Sources

So the jokes have footnotes

Read these. Then argue with the calculator.

  1. [01]

    Ferreira, M., Domingos, I., Santos, L., Barreto, A., and coauthors. Environmental and Economic Assessment of Desktop vs. Laptop Computers: A Life Cycle Approach. Sustainability 2025, 17(10), 4455. Laptop total 286.1 kg CO₂e over four years; main-unit production 218.7 kg; use 59.4 kg. Desktop system 679.1 kg, of which the display alone was about 400 kg.
    mdpi.com/2071-1050/17/10/4455 ↗

  2. [02]

    German Environment Agency (Umweltbundesamt), with Öko-Institut and Fraunhofer IZM. Timely replacement of a notebook under consideration of environmental aspects. Production about 56% of life-cycle greenhouse gases in the studied notebook scenario (about 214 kg CO₂e total over five years). A 10% efficiency gain did not pay back manufacturing on a realistic replacement cycle; amortisation ran about 33 to 89 years across scenarios. UBA publication 4317.

  3. [03]

    André, H., Ljunggren Söderman, M., and Nordelöf, A. Resource and environmental impacts of using second-hand laptop computers: A case study of commercial reuse. Waste Management 2019. Preparation for reuse was negligible next to the benefit of extending use.
    doi.org/10.1016/j.wasman.2019.03.050 ↗

  4. [04]

    ADEME, 2022 assessment of refurbished equipment, as summarized by Fraunhofer IZM (November 2022): on a cut-off basis, using a refurbished laptop was estimated about 77% lower in greenhouse gases than using a new one (24 kg CO₂e versus 178 kg, on ADEME’s lifespans). Fraunhofer IZM summary.

  5. [05]

    Circular and Fair ICT Pact. Practical Strategies for Carbon-Conscious ICT Procurement, 2025. Cites an “around 80% before use” figure to an Open University briefing, and cites Sutton-Parker and Procter, Procedia Computer Science, 2023, for up to a 37.5% emissions cut from keeping computers an extra two years. CFIT mini guide.

  6. [06]

    ITU and UNITAR. Global E-waste Monitor 2024. 62 billion kg of e-waste generated in 2022; 22.3% documented as formally collected and recycled.
    ewastemonitor.info ↗

  7. [07]

    Gartner. Best Practices for Device Sustainability in End-User Computing, 13 March 2024 — public summary: most organizations are moving employee laptops toward four- and five-year refresh. And: Extend Hardware Budgets By Giving PCs a Second Life, 3 December 2025 — pressure to go to five years and beyond, usually with a midlife refurbishment.
    gartner.com/en/documents/5274363 ↗ gartner.com/en/documents/7238830 ↗

  8. [08]

    Reuse versus recycling, up to 20×: stated in public by Sims Lifecycle Services as a finding of Gartner’s Market Guide for IT Asset Disposition. The guide itself is paid. Treat the multiple as Gartner’s, via that quotation, not as a GLOW Ops measurement. Sims note on the 2024 guide.

  9. [09]

    About 30% of IT hardware lost, missing, or ghosted: repeated by ITAM and ITAD firms (including Juriba and Iron Mountain) as a Gartner finding. Secondary citations. The report’s ghost flag uses the tenant’s own check-in data, not this percentage.
